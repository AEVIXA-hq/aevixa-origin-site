THE THINKING 01 / 03

Less noise.
More signal.

Security findings are only useful when you can understand why they exist. Origin starts with a simple principle: every lead should point back to something concrete.

Product 001 explores deterministic Python checks and source-linked evidence. It doesn't pretend that automated detection is a full security assessment.
