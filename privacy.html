CONTACT & PRIVACY

Know where your message goes.

Last updated 6 November 2026. This page describes the informational Product 001 website and its contact options.

Website and local planner

The current static site has no custom analytics, custom cookies, site database, uploading or server-backed contact form. The optional scope planner runs locally in your browser. Hosting and network providers, including Cloudflare, may process technical request logs under their own terms.

Enquiring by email

An email button opens your chosen email client addressed to aevixahq@gmail.com. Nothing is transmitted by our site. If you decide to send, your email provider and the receiving mailbox handle the address, message, and replies under their own privacy terms. Please send only information you are authorized to share and avoid private code, credentials or sensitive personal information. Email handling is not automatically linked to Origin Core.

GitHub public interest form

The optional GitHub issue form creates a public issue under AEVIXA-hq/aevixa-origin-site. GitHub requires sign-in. Your GitHub handle and issue content can be visible, searchable, and retained under GitHub's terms. Do not post personal contact information, private repositories, code, keys, or other confidential details. The issue is only an unverified interest signal, not identity confirmation or consent to scan.

Work and payments

Creating a scope draft, emailing, or filing a public issue does not start a scan, order, contract, invoice, or payment. Any future permitted work requires explicit confirmed arrangements and authorization. This website does not accept payments.

Contact

For questions about your email correspondence, contact aevixahq@gmail.com. Public GitHub issue posts should be edited or removed through GitHub controls; copies or prior indexing may remain.
